# OPEN CLAIMS CLOSURE CHECKLIST

Status: ACTIVE  
Trigger class: Claim-boundary change  
Governed by: `CHANGE_CONTROL.md`

---

## Purpose

This checklist must be completed before moving a claim from UNHELD to CLOSED.

---

## Required checks

- evidence is externally verifiable
- BUILD_RECEIPTS entry prepared
- CHANGELOG entry prepared
- OPEN_CLAIMS section movement prepared
- governance index update considered
- closure does not rely on local receipt alone
- closure does not rely on social consensus
- boundary statement preserved

---

## Standing boundary

This checklist governs closure readiness only.

It does not itself close a claim.
