# Category Boundary Test v0.1

**Status:** `DRAFT / NON_EXEC / CLAIM_BOUNDARY_SEED`

A proof surface may be legitimate without proving the full category.

This repository demonstrates a path-local commit boundary.

It does not claim path-universal coverage, production readiness, certification, deployment, or a full runtime fabric.

---

## Purpose

This note separates two evidential burdens that are easy to collapse in public governance discussion:

1. a narrow proof-surface claim
2. a full category claim

They are not the same claim.

---

## Two different claims

### 1. Narrow proof-surface claim

A bounded artifact proves one invariant on one demonstrated path.

For `commit-gate-core`, that claim is:

> No consequence at this commit boundary without a valid signed, scoped, unexpired, unreplayed DecisionRecord.

This is a path-local invariant.

### 2. Full category claim

A system claims standing across:

- changed conditions
- consequence formation
- replay
- refusal
- receipt
- runtime fabric
- deployment placement
- path-universal coverage

That claim has a wider evidential burden.

---

## Minimum category test

Before treating any artifact as a full category claim, ask:

- Where does admissibility resolve?
- Where does consequence begin forming?
- What is refused under changed conditions?
- What receipt proves the boundary decision?
- What replay reproduces the decision?
- Is the claim path-local or path-universal?
- Is there deployment evidence, or only a proof surface?
- Are bypass paths, side channels, retries, rollbacks, and handoffs in scope?

---

## Claim boundary

`commit-gate-core` is a bounded partial proof surface.

It is not ownership of the category.

It is not a full AI governance system.

It is not path-universal coverage.

It is one inspectable brick.

---

## Public wording rule

Safe:

> This demonstrates one path-local invariant on the demonstrated path.

Unsafe:

> This proves the whole category.

Safe:

> Refusal can produce an auditable receipt when the audit sink accepts the event.

Unsafe:

> Refusal always leaves a receipt.

Safe:

> This is a bounded proof surface.

Unsafe:

> This is a complete runtime governance fabric.
